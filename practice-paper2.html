Part 1 · Email · 20 marks

You received an email from your cousin, Ani, who wants your opinion on travelling.

Hi,

I feel that I want to celebrate with my classmates after SPM and we decided to travel somewhere relaxing. Do you think it is better for us to travel locally or overseas? Why do you say so? How much should we spend for our travels? Are you interested in joining us? We are happy to have you with us.

Write to me soon.

Bye!
Ani

Write an email to your cousin in about 80 words.

Part 2 · Guided Essay · 20 marks

Your class has been discussing how to get extra income after SPM. Your teacher has asked you to write an essay on it.

In your essay, you should include:
• the ways to get extra income
• the challenges teenagers face in getting extra income
• how additional money could help teenagers

Use all the notes above and give reasons for your point of view.
Write your essay in 125-150 words in an appropriate style.

Part 3 · Extended Writing · 20 marks

Answer one question. Write 200-250 words in an appropriate style.

Question 3
Your teacher has asked you to write a story for a story writing competition. The story must begin with the following words:

Farah was being chased by a dark shadow. Luckily, it was just a dream...

In your story, you must include:
• a description of the dream and how she reacted to it
• how it affected Farah's life

Write your story.
Question 4
You see this notice on your school notice board.

Articles wanted!
Sleep - you will feel great!
Sleep is very important to us. How long should we sleep per day?
What should we do before we sleep? Tell us what are the benefits of having enough sleep.
The best article will receive a gift voucher from the English Panel.

Write your article.
Question 5
You see this notice on your school website.

Your opinion matters!
A new energy drink has just been launched. Write and tell us your thoughts.
• Name the drink.
• What do you like about it?
• Would you recommend the energy drink to your friends? Why?
Share with us your review on it. The most interesting review will be featured on our website.

Write your review.
