Q1

Hardness of Alloy vs Pure Metal 合金与纯金属硬度

5 marks
a · 1 mark(s)
Question Focus / 题目重点： State a fixed factor / State faktor yang ditetapkan
Official Answer / 官方答案
Mass of the weight / height from which the weight is dropped / size of the steel ball / size of the block.
Explanation / 华语解释
可接受的固定因素包括：砝码质量、砝码掉落高度、钢球大小或金属块大小。题目要的是 controlled variable（固定变量），写其中一个即可。
b · 1 mark(s)
Question Focus / 题目重点： Write one observation
Official Answer / 官方答案
The diameter of the dent on the bronze block is smaller than the diameter of the dent on the copper block.
Explanation / 华语解释
观察必须只写看到的数据现象：青铜块的凹痕直径比铜块小。不要在 observation 加入“因为更硬”，那属于 inference。
c · 1 mark(s)
Question Focus / 题目重点： State an inference / State inferens
Official Answer / 官方答案
The diameter of the dent on the bronze block is smaller because bronze is harder than copper.
Explanation / 华语解释
Inference 要把观察连到原因：青铜凹痕较小，因为青铜比铜更硬。关键词是 kerana / because。
d · 1 mark(s)
Question Focus / 题目重点： Operational definition of alloy
Official Answer / 官方答案
An alloy is a material that produces a smaller dent when a 1 kg weight is dropped onto a steel ball placed on it.
Explanation / 华语解释
操作性定义必须根据本实验如何测量“alloy”：1 kg砝码落下后，形成较小凹痕的材料。
e · 1 mark(s)
Question Focus / 题目重点： Explain the larger dent when 2 kg weight is used
Official Answer / 官方答案
The dent diameter is larger because a weight with a greater mass produces a greater force when dropped onto the steel ball, thus producing a larger dent.
Explanation / 华语解释
完整链条：质量较大 → 产生较大的力 → 钢球压入更深 → 凹痕直径更大。

Question Focus / 题目重点： Explain A, B and C in Venturi tube
Official Answer / 官方答案
A: low velocity, high pressure. B: high velocity, low pressure. C: moderate velocity, moderate pressure. Any four correct points.
Explanation / 华语解释
把每个位置的velocity与pressure配对。官方六个点中任意4个正确即可。
d · 4 mark(s)
Question Focus / 题目重点： Justify the safety line at a railway station
Official Answer / 官方答案
Justified. Air flows at high velocity and the air pressure is low in the region between a fast-moving train and a nearby person. A person standing beyond the safety line may be pushed by a force towards the moving train.
Explanation / 华语解释
4个评分点：合理/需要安全线；高速气流；低压区；越线的人可能受到力而被推向行驶中的火车。
